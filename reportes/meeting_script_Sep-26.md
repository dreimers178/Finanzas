# Portal Metrics — Monthly Meeting (Sep - 26)
### Speaking script — Collections team (~2 min)

---

## Opening

> "Thanks everyone. Quick rundown of our portal numbers for September — this is the whole Collections team's work: getting our clients' invoices into each customer's portal so we get paid. Scope: everything created through September 30, with a 2-day SLA window to resolve (so completions counted through October 2)."

## Portal Uploads

> "First, Portal Uploads — the upload requests we handled.
> - **85 uploads**, **73 resolved**, at **0.7 days** on average and **85% same-day**.
> - And it's a clear trend: **2.9 days in July → 1.7 in August → 0.7 in September**. We keep getting faster.
> - By platform, Coupa and Ariba carry most of our volume. The slowest is Chorus Pro (~5 days), and the URL-based cases remain our access opportunity — no standing login set up."

## Tesorio Tasks

> "Second, Tesorio Tasks — how we track that same work end to end.
> - **240 tasks created**, **204 completed** within the 2-day SLA window, at **1.1 days** on average and **67% same-day**.
> - **38 were still open** past the window.
> - The 2-day window matters because a lot of tasks were created right at month-end (Sep 30) and closed on Oct 1-2 — those count as on-time, which is why September's average sits at just over a day."

## ⭐ The September spike — what to watch

> "One important note: September saw a **big jump in task volume**. The reason wasn't demand — it's that **many invoices were already ready, with the PO in place and everything set to submit**, but the **follow-up to actually create the task / raise the request wasn't happening**. For a couple of weeks that backed up and became critical, and that's what drove the spike.
>
> So the ask for the team: let's **stay on top of raising those requests as the invoices become ready**, instead of letting them pile up. If we keep the flow **steady month to month** rather than in bursts, we can submit invoices on time and **avoid overloading the portals team** in certain months."

## Quick reminder — reassigned tasks

> "One quick reminder: some Tesorio tasks I send back and reassign to the collector because something's off with the invoice — missing data, missing info, or no purchase order. Please keep an eye on those and clear them up promptly, so the SLA doesn't keep stretching. Just something to stay on top of and check regularly."

## Closing

> "To sum up: faster every month, most resolved same-day, and one clear habit to fix — raise the requests steadily so we don't create these peaks. Thank you all — great month. Questions?"
