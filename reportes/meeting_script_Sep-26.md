# Portal Metrics — Monthly Meeting (Sep - 26)
### Speaking script — short (~1.5 min)

---

## Opening

> "Thanks everyone. A quick look at our portal numbers for September — this is the team's work getting our clients' invoices into each customer's portal so we get paid on time. Scope is through September 30, with our 2-day SLA window to resolve."

## The numbers

> "Portal Uploads: **85 uploads, 73 resolved**, at **0.7 days** on average and **85% same-day** — and the trend keeps improving month over month.
>
> On Tesorio Tasks we **completed 204 tasks** at **1.1 days**, **67% same-day**, with 38 still in flight. Overall, a strong and faster month for the team."

## September volume

> "One thing to flag: September volume jumped. A lot of invoices became ready to submit around the same time — POs in place and good to go. The opportunity is simply to **smooth that intake** so the work comes in steadily month to month rather than in a wave — that keeps us submitting on time and avoids peaks for the portals team."

## Friendly reminders

> "Two quick reminders:
> - I really want to put a spotlight on **Monto's PO report**, shared in our team Slack channel. This is a great tool, and it goes straight at **one of the biggest pain points we've had — getting the POs**. It pulls the POs right from the portals we use, so **if a client hasn't sent you the PO, you can find it there first** instead of chasing it. Please lean on it — it saves real time and keeps invoices moving.
> - And since September brought such a big volume, it's also a good moment to **review the tasks the portals team had to reassign** because something was missing — a PO or some information. Keeping an eye on those and clearing them up makes the whole process **smoother** and keeps the SLA tight."

## Closing

> "So: faster every month, and a couple of small habits to keep it steady. Thanks, team — great month. Happy to take questions."
