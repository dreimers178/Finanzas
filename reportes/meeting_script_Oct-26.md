# Portal Metrics — Monthly Meeting (Oct - 26)
### Speaking script — short (~1.5 min)

---

## Opening

> "Thanks everyone. A quick look at our portal numbers for October — this is the team's work getting our clients' invoices into each customer's portal so we get paid on time. Scope is through October 31, with our 2-day SLA window to resolve."

## The numbers

> "On **Portal Uploads** it was a strong month: **61 uploads, 56 resolved**, at **0.6 days** on average and **88% same-day** — and the trend keeps improving, from 1.7 days in August to 0.7 in September to 0.6 in October.
>
> On **Tesorio Tasks** we **completed 164 tasks**, with only 5 still open. It was a heavier month on turnaround — **6.3 days** on average and **31% same-day** — because a large batch of invoices came in together in mid-October and took about two weeks to clear, which pulls the average up."

## What drove October

> "The takeaway is the same one as last month: when a lot of work lands at once, turnaround stretches. The opportunity is to **smooth that intake** so requests come in steadily rather than in a wave — that keeps turnaround tight and avoids peaks for the portals team."

## Friendly reminders

> "Two quick reminders:
> - I really want to put a spotlight on **Monto's PO report**, shared in our team Slack channel. This is a great tool, and it goes straight at **one of the biggest pain points we've had — getting the POs**. It pulls the POs right from the portals we use, so **if a client hasn't sent you the PO, you can find it there first** instead of chasing it. Please lean on it — it saves real time and keeps invoices moving.
> - And with the higher volume, it's a good moment to **review the tasks the portals team had to reassign** because something was missing — a PO or some information. Clearing those quickly keeps the process smoother and the SLA tight."

## Closing

> "So: a strong month on uploads, a heavier one on turnaround that points to one clear habit — keep the intake steady. Thanks, team. Happy to take questions."
